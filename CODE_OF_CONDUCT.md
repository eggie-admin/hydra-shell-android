# Code of Conduct

LuHm OS / KAI 9000 is a collaborative engineering and creative project. Participation should make the project easier to build, test, understand, and enjoy.

## Expected
- Be respectful and specific in technical disagreement.
- Critique code, architecture, evidence, and decisions rather than people.
- Document failures honestly.
- Respect privacy, licenses, attribution, and security boundaries.
- Keep reviews focused on the work.
- Make reasonable room for different communication styles and accessibility needs.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sexual harassment.
- Publishing private information or secrets.
- Knowingly falsifying build/test/release evidence.
- Deliberately inserting malicious code, credential theft, hidden telemetry, or policy bypasses.
- Using project infrastructure to target or intimidate others.

## Enforcement
Repository maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

Security-sensitive behavior should be handled through `SECURITY.md`, not public argument.
